Admission Requirements

To apply for admission to Shree Hunainath Educational Academy (Nursery to Grade 8), students and guardians must meet the following criteria:

  • Age Criteria: Children must meet the age eligibility standards specified by the Ministry of Education & Shuklaphanta Municipality (Montessori/Nursery: 3+ years, Grade 1: 5+ years).
  • Previous Academic Records: Progress report card, character certificate, or latest marksheet from the previous institution (applicable for Grade 1 through Grade 8).
  • Official Birth Certificate: Copy of the official birth registration certificate issued by the Ward Office / Local Registrar (पञ्जीकाधिकारी कार्यालय).
  • Passport-Sized Photographs: 3 recent color passport-sized photographs of the student and 1 photograph each of parents/guardian.
  • Parent / Guardian Identification: Copy of Nepali Citizenship (नागरिकता), National ID, or valid passport of father/mother/legal guardian.
  • Transfer Certificate & IMEIS: Transfer Certificate (TC/SLC) and online Integrated Educational Management Information System (IEMIS) student transfer record for Grade 2 and above.
Important Note: Admissions are conducted on a first-come, first-served merit basis subject to classroom seat availability and interaction evaluation.

Fee Structure & Scholarships

Hunainath Educational Academy operates under transparent, affordable fee structures adhering to Shuklaphanta Municipality Education Guidelines. Detailed fee sheets per grade level are provided at the admissions desk.

  • One-time Admission Fee: Payable solely upon initial enrollment into the academy.
  • Monthly Tuition Fee: Modest installments structured with multi-child family considerations.
  • Co-Curricular & Computer Lab: Practical IT instruction, smart-board learning, sports, and library access included.
  • Transportation / School Bus: Optional safe daily pick-up & drop-off service across Garjamuni, Jhalari, and neighbouring routes.
Merit & Need-Based Scholarships: Hunainath offers partial and full tuition scholarships for underprivileged, marginalized community students, martyr families, and academic toppers. Contact our administration office for the scholarship application form.
