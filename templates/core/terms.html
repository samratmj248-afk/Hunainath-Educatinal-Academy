Official Academy Policies

Terms & Conditions

Institutional guidelines, student code of conduct, admission criteria, and digital services policy for Shree Hunainath Educational Academy.

Effective: Academic Session 2082–2083 B.S.

1. Institutional Scope & Scope of Operation

Shree Hunainath Educational Academy is an officially recognized educational institution affiliated with the Ministry of Education and Shuklaphanta Municipality Education Department. The institution currently provides academic instruction from Early Childhood Development (Montessori Nursery, LKG, UKG) through Grade 8 (Basic Level Education - BLE).

2. Admissions & Student Enrollment

  • Admissions into ECD to Grade 8 are granted subject to seat availability, document verification, and age criteria set by educational regulations.
  • Parents/Guardians must furnish authentic certificates (birth registration, previous academic grade sheets, transfer certificates). Providing false information will lead to cancellation of enrollment.
  • The online admission portal allows tracking review status; final admission is confirmed upon fee clearance and verification by the Principal.

3. Attendance, Punctuality & Leave Rules

A minimum of 80% attendance is strictly required for students to be eligible for Terminal and Annual Board Examinations. In case of illness or unforeseen absence, guardians must submit a written leave application or notify the school administration promptly.

4. Examination Rules & Results Portal

Terminal and Annual Examinations follow standard grading scales (GPA system) approved by the Ministry of Education. The online Exam Results Portal is provided as an official communication channel for guardians. Any tampering or unauthorized data extraction is strictly prohibited.

5. Student Code of Conduct & School Safety

  • Students must arrive in proper clean school uniform with school ID card daily.
  • Respect for teachers, staff, peers, and school property is mandatory. Bullying, physical altercations, and inappropriate behavior carry strict disciplinary actions.
  • Electronic gadgets or unnecessary valuables are prohibited on school premises unless specifically authorized for educational projects.
